# Where these photographs came from

The demo needs pictures of real equipment, and these are real: three
photographs from Wikimedia Commons, each under a licence that permits
commercial use without attribution. They are credited here anyway, because
knowing the provenance of a binary in a repository is worth more than the
licence strictly requires.

Each file is the 1200px rendition served by Wikimedia rather than the original,
which is why they are a few hundred kilobytes instead of several megabytes.

If Kreworx is ever shown to a client as their own system, replace these with
photographs of that client's own work. They are here to make a demo honest,
not because they belong to this business.
